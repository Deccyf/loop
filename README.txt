LOOP: THE INSTALLABLE VERSION
=============================

This folder is the whole app. Put it on any web host that serves files over https,
then open its address on your phone and add it to your home screen.

PUTTING IT ON GITHUB PAGES

1. On github.com, create a new public repository, for example "loop".
2. Upload everything in this folder to it, keeping the folders as they are:
   index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, and the icons and vendor folders.
3. In the repository, open Settings, then Pages.
   Under "Build and deployment", choose "Deploy from a branch", pick the main branch
   and the / (root) folder, and save.
4. After a minute or two the site is live at
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/loop/

ADDING IT TO YOUR HOME SCREEN

iPhone:  open the address in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android: open the address in Chrome, open the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

Open Loop once while you're online, so the phone can store it. After that it works offline.

GOOD TO KNOW

- Progress is kept on the phone, not in an account. Profile and settings has
  "Save a backup" and "Restore a backup" for keeping it safe or moving it.
- The AI tutor only works in the version inside Claude, so it's hidden here.
- To update: replace the files with a newer copy of this folder. Phones pick up
  the new version the next time they open Loop with a connection, and use it from
  the time after that.
